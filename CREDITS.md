# Credits

Tone Generator is a Windows desktop test-tone generator: continuous sine, square, triangle, and sawtooth tones, frequency presets from 20 Hz to 15 kHz, loudest-frequency finding, WASAPI loopback measurement, and playback through any chosen output device.

Third-party projects it uses:

- wxPython for the interface.
- NumPy for the signals.
- sounddevice (and its PortAudio DLLs) for playback.
